# 💜 Agathamudi Himabindu — Professional Portfolio Website

A responsive, high-performance portfolio website built with semantic **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **vanilla JavaScript**, styled in an elegant **Royal Amethyst & Electric Violet** color theme.

Customized based on the official resume of **Agathamudi Himabindu**, B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering Undergraduate (Class of 2027) at Bhaskar Engineering College.

---

## 🎨 Theme & Styling
- **Theme Palette:** Royal Amethyst & Electric Violet
- **Primary Colors:**
  - Electric Violet: `#a855f7`
  - Royal Purple: `#8b5cf6`
  - Neon Lilac Glow: `#c084fc`
  - Rose Violet: `#f472b6`
  - Deep Velvet Background: `#0c0716`
  - Surface Glass: `#140d25`
- **Glassmorphism:** Frosted glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with glowing borders.
- **Ambient Stargaze Canvas:** Dynamic particle constellation engine connecting stars across the canvas.
- **Dark/Light Mode:** Seamless theme toggling between deep midnight purple and soft lavender light mode, remembered across sessions with `localStorage`.
